# Sponsorship platform icons

These platform marks identify external sponsorship links. They remain the
property of their respective owners and are not covered by CrossDesk's own
artwork license. Retrieved on 2026-10-19.

- `afdian.png`: [AFDIAN](https://afdian.com/) navigation logo, downloaded from
  [its official CDN](https://static.afdiancdn.com/static/img/logo/logo.png)
  without modification (80 × 80).
- `kofi.png`: cup icon from [Ko-fi's brand assets](https://more.ko-fi.com/brand-assets),
  downloaded from [its official asset host](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5c14e387dab576fe667689cf/670f5a01229bf8a18f97a3c1_favion.avif),
  converted to PNG and proportionally resized to 64 × 64.
- `github.svg`: unmodified `GitHub_Invertocat_Black.svg` from the
  [official logo archive](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip).
  [GitHub's brand guidelines](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo)
  permit the logo to identify a link to GitHub.
